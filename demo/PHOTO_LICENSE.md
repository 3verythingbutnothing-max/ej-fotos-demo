# Demo photography

These bundled images are Unsplash stock photographs for software demonstration only. They are not EJFoto client images or an EJFoto portfolio. Download URLs are recorded in provenance.json. License reviewed 2026-10-05: https://unsplash.com/license. Do not sell these samples as prints. The sample files are resized demo fixtures; guest downloads preserve each submitted image's pixel dimensions but cannot restore missing camera resolution. The application runs without fetching stock photos at runtime.
